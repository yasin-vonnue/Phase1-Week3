Company History

1998
The Foundation: The company was founded in a small garage focused on developing core technology for early-stage digital projects.
2002
First Major Expansion: Successfully launched our flagship product, expanding operations beyond the initial local market.
2006
Series A Funding: Secured major institutional funding, allowing for rapid engineering team expansion and global market testing.
2020
Global Headquarters: Moved to our current central office to accommodate a workforce that had grown by four times in just three years.
2024
AI Integration: Launched our predictive-intelligence engine, streamlining user experiences and automating complex analytics.
